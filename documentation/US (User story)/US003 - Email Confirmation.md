## Story Statement

` I, as an user, want to to be able to confirm my own email by my first 2FA or by my own.`

## Acceptance Criteria

- User needs to confirm your accoutn with confirmation email or 2FA

## Technical notes

- The user should have the ability to trigger a confirmation of their own email address.
- The user will automatically confirm their email if they log in using 2FA.
